## To the latest revision

Press <KeyCap key="u" /> anywhere. `revision` confirms first, then runs `svn update` and
shows a progress dialog naming where the working copy is coming from and going to.

## To a specific revision

Focus the Log panel with <KeyCap key="3" />, select a revision, and press
<KeyCap key="space" />. This works in both directions: forwards to catch up, or backwards
to put the working copy at an older revision.

The asterisk in the Log panel marks where the working copy currently sits, so you can see
what you are moving from.

## What happens to your changes

Both paths keep uncommitted work. SVN merges the incoming revision into your modified
files rather than discarding them, exactly as `svn update` does on the command line.

If any file is **already conflicted**, a second prompt says so before anything runs: SVN
leaves conflicted files untouched and updates the rest. Resolve them with `svn resolve` —
`revision` does not have a conflict resolver yet.

## After it finishes

- The Status panel's `Revision` line moves to the new revision.
- The Log panel's asterisk moves with it.
- Status and history reload, so the Files panel reflects any merged changes.
- The `svn update` invocation is recorded in the
  [Command Log](/guides/panels/#command-log), marked ✓ or ✗.

Press <KeyCap key="R" /> at any time to re-read status and history without updating.

## Authentication

`svn update` is the operation most likely to need credentials. `revision` always runs
`svn` with `--non-interactive`, so instead of hanging on a hidden prompt it fails fast
with an actionable hint. See [Authentication](/operations/authentication/).

## Updating revision itself

<KeyCap key="u" /> updates the *working copy*. To update the `revision` binary, see
[Updating revision](/operations/updating-revision/).
