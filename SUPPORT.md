# Support

## Documentation first

Most questions are answered on the documentation site:

- [Quick start](https://bapatchirag.github.io/revision/guides/quick-start/) — the first
  five minutes.
- [Keybindings](https://bapatchirag.github.io/revision/reference/keybindings/) — every key,
  also available in the app with `?`.
- [Configuration](https://bapatchirag.github.io/revision/reference/configuration/) — the
  config file and its defaults.
- [Troubleshooting](https://bapatchirag.github.io/revision/operations/troubleshooting/) —
  common failures and their fixes.
- [FAQ](https://bapatchirag.github.io/revision/faq/) — what `revision` touches, where it
  runs, and why it exists.

The command log panel at the bottom of the app shows every `svn` command `revision` runs
and its result. It is usually the fastest way to see what actually went wrong.

## Still stuck

Open an [issue](https://github.com/bapatchirag/revision/issues) — questions are welcome
there, not just bugs. Include your `revision --version`, `svn --version`, OS, and terminal.

For a suspected security vulnerability, follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead
of opening an issue.

## Contributing a fix

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

`revision` is maintained by one person in their spare time. Replies may take a while.
